# Ollama Recovery

## Background

After a Linux Mint filesystem failure, I worked to restore my local Ollama environment on Linux Mint 22.3.

## Recovery milestones

According to my recovery notes, I:

- Reinstalled Ollama.
- Verified that the Ollama service was active and running.
- Verified access to the local API.
- Restored the Ollama configuration from my backup.
- Downloaded models again because the previous model files were not present in the restored configuration.

## Outcome

I tested two Qwen 2.5 model sizes after the recovery. Both generated responses, allowing me to continue exploring local AI and comparing their answers.
